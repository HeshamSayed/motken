# Data Deletion Instructions for Motken

**Last Updated: November 7, 2025**

## Overview

Motken respects your right to privacy and provides multiple ways to delete your account and personal data. This document explains how to request deletion of your data in compliance with GDPR, CCPA, and app store requirements.

## How to Delete Your Account

### Option 1: In-App Deletion

1. **Open the Motken app**
2. **Navigate to Profile** → Tap the Profile icon in the bottom navigation
3. **Go to Settings** → Tap the Settings icon in the top right
4. **Scroll to Account** → Find "Account Management" section
5. **Tap "Delete Account"**
6. **Confirm deletion** → Read the warning and tap "Delete My Account"
7. **Enter password** → Confirm with your password
8. **Receive confirmation** → You'll get an email confirming deletion

###Option 2: Email Request

Send an email to **privacy@motken.com** with:
- **Subject**: "Account Deletion Request"
- **Include**:
  - Your registered email address
  - Your full name
  - Account type (Student/Teacher/Parent)
  - Reason for deletion (optional)

**Example Email:**
```
To: privacy@motken.com
Subject: Account Deletion Request

I request the deletion of my Motken account associated with:
Email: john.doe@example.com
Name: John Doe
Account Type: Student

Please confirm when my data has been deleted.

Thank you,
John Doe
```

### Option 3: Website Request

1. Visit **https://motken.com/delete-account**
2. Log in with your credentials
3. Fill out the deletion request form
4. Verify your identity via email
5. Confirm deletion

## What Data Gets Deleted

### Immediately Deleted
- **Profile information** (name, email, phone, photo)
- **Session recordings** (all recordings deleted immediately)
- **Chat messages** (all conversation history)
- **Learning progress** (lessons, scores, achievements)
- **Device tokens** (push notification tokens)
- **Preferences** (settings, notifications)

### Retained for Legal Compliance (Anonymized)
- **Transaction records** (required for tax/accounting - 7 years)
- **Payment receipts** (anonymized invoices)
- **Session attendance logs** (for teacher payouts - 90 days)

### Never Deleted
- **Aggregated analytics data** (anonymized, no personal identifiers)
- **Public reviews** (anonymized, name removed but content kept)

## Timeline

| Action | Timeline |
|--------|----------|
| **Request acknowledgment** | Within 24 hours |
| **Account deactivation** | Immediate |
| **Data deletion completion** | Within 30 days |
| **Confirmation email** | After deletion completes |

## Before You Delete

### Warning: Deletion is Permanent!

Once deleted, you **cannot recover**:
- Your profile and learning history
- Unused session credits
- Subscription benefits
- Achievements and certificates

### Alternatives to Consider

Instead of deleting, you can:
- **Deactivate account** (temporarily disable)
- **Cancel subscription** (keep account, stop billing)
- **Delete specific data** (e.g., only recordings)
- **Update privacy settings** (limit data collection)

## Special Cases

### For Children Under 18

Parents/guardians must:
- Make the deletion request on behalf of the child
- Verify relationship to the child
- Provide consent for deletion

**Email template:**
```
I am the parent/guardian of [Child's Name] and request deletion
of their account: [child@email.com]

Parent Name: [Your Name]
Verification: [Attached ID or verification code]
```

### For Teachers

Teachers must:
- Complete all pending sessions
- Resolve any payment issues
- Download payout records (if needed)

**Processing time**: Up to 45 days to complete payouts

### For Parents with Family Accounts

- Deleting parent account deletes all linked children accounts
- Each child account must be deleted separately if keeping parent account

## Data Deletion Confirmation

### What You'll Receive

After deletion, you'll receive:
1. **Immediate confirmation** email
2. **7-day reminder** email (option to cancel deletion)
3. **Final confirmation** email after 30 days

### Verification

You can verify deletion by:
- Attempting to log in (should fail)
- Requesting data export (should return no data)
- Contacting support for confirmation

## Canceling a Deletion Request

### During Grace Period (7 Days)

If you change your mind within 7 days:
1. Reply to the confirmation email
2. Or email **privacy@motken.com**
3. Your account will be reactivated

### After Grace Period

After 7 days, deletion cannot be canceled.

## Regional Requirements

### GDPR (European Union)

EU residents have the right to:
- Request data deletion ("Right to be Forgotten")
- Receive deletion within 30 days
- Lodge complaint with supervisory authority

**Contact**: dpo@motken.com (Data Protection Officer)

### CCPA (California)

California residents can:
- Request deletion of personal information
- Receive confirmation of deletion
- Not face discrimination for exercising rights

### COPPA (United States - Children)

Parents of children under 13 can:
- Request deletion at any time
- Receive immediate confirmation
- Prevent further data collection

## Technical Details

### What Happens Behind the Scenes

1. **Day 0**: Request received, account deactivated
2. **Day 1-7**: Grace period (can cancel)
3. **Day 8**: Automated deletion process starts
4. **Day 30**: All personal data permanently deleted
5. **Day 31**: Confirmation email sent

### Data Deletion Methods

- **Soft delete**: 7-day grace period
- **Hard delete**: Permanent removal from databases
- **Anonymization**: Strip personal identifiers
- **Backup purge**: Remove from all backups

## Exceptions

### We May Retain Data If:
- Required by law (legal proceedings)
- Necessary for fraud prevention
- Needed to complete pending transactions
- Part of ongoing investigation

**Note**: You'll be notified if we must retain any data.
