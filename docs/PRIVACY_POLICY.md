# Privacy Policy for Motken

**Last Updated: November 7, 2025**

## Introduction

Motken ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Motken mobile application and website (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our Service.

## Information We Collect

### Personal Information
When you register for our Service, we collect:
- **Name** (first name and last name)
- **Email address**
- **Phone number** (optional)
- **Country of residence**
- **Profile photo** (optional)
- **Age/Date of birth** (for parental consent if under 13/16)
- **Payment information** (processed securely through Paymob)

### Usage Information
We automatically collect:
- **Device information** (device type, operating system, unique device identifiers)
- **Log data** (IP address, access times, pages viewed)
- **Session data** (attendance, duration, recordings with consent)
- **Learning progress** (lessons completed, quiz scores, achievements)

### Camera and Microphone Access
We request camera and microphone permissions for:
- **Video sessions** with teachers via Zoom integration
- Access is only requested when needed for sessions
- You can deny these permissions, but video calling features will be unavailable

## How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:
- **Provide the Service**: Facilitate Quran learning sessions between students and teachers
- **Process payments**: Handle subscription payments and teacher payouts
- **Communication**: Send session reminders, updates, and notifications
- **Improve the Service**: Analyze usage patterns and enhance features
- **Safety and security**: Protect against fraud and unauthorized access
- **Legal compliance**: Comply with applicable laws and regulations

## Information Sharing

We DO NOT sell your personal information. We share information only:
- **With teachers**: Your name and learning preferences when booking sessions
- **With payment processors**: Paymob for payment processing
- **With service providers**: Zoom for video calling, AWS for storage
- **For legal requirements**: When required by law or to protect rights
- **With your consent**: When you explicitly authorize sharing

## Children's Privacy

### Age Requirements
- **Minimum age**: 13 years old (16 in EU)
- **Parental consent**: Required for users under 18

### Parental Controls
Parents/guardians can:
- Monitor their child's sessions and progress
- Control privacy settings
- Request data deletion
- Contact us at privacy@motken.com

### Data Collection from Children
We collect only necessary information and:
- Do NOT collect more data from children than necessary
- Do NOT share children's data with third parties for marketing
- Parents can review and delete their child's information

## Data Storage and Security

### Security Measures
- **Encryption**: Data encrypted in transit (SSL/TLS) and at rest
- **Authentication**: JWT tokens with secure refresh mechanism
- **Access controls**: Role-based access to limit data exposure
- **Regular audits**: Security assessments and vulnerability testing

### Data Retention
- **Account data**: Retained while account is active
- **Session recordings**: Stored for 90 days, then deleted
- **Payment data**: Retained per legal requirements (7 years)
- **Deleted account data**: Anonymized after 30 days

## Your Privacy Rights

### Access and Control
You have the right to:
- **Access** your personal information
- **Correct** inaccurate information
- **Delete** your account and data
- **Export** your data in portable format
- **Opt-out** of marketing communications
- **Withdraw consent** for data processing

### Regional Rights
- **GDPR (EU)**: Right to data portability, right to be forgotten
- **CCPA (California)**: Right to opt-out of data sale (we don't sell data)
- **COPPA (US)**: Parental rights for children under 13

### Exercising Your Rights
Contact us at privacy@motken.com to:
- Request data access or deletion
- Update your information
- Lodge a complaint

## Data Deletion

### How to Delete Your Account
1. **In-app**: Go to Profile > Settings > Delete Account
2. **Email**: Send request to privacy@motken.com
3. **Response time**: Within 30 days

### What Happens When You Delete
- Personal information deleted within 30 days
- Session recordings deleted immediately
- Some data retained for legal compliance (invoices, transactions)
- Anonymized data may be retained for analytics
